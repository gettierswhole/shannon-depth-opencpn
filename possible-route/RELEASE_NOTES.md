**Beta. Possible route, not an aid to navigation.** A standalone Windows tool, separate from the Shannon Depth plugin.

Lough Ree only (Athlone to Lanesborough, including Athlone lock). It needs your own copy of the CSIG chart and an internet connection for today's levels.

Unzip it, then run `PossibleRoute.exe`. Windows will warn that the beta isn't signed: choose **More info → Run anyway**. See README.md.

Known limits in this beta:
- Places outside Lough Ree, and the reaches beyond Tarmonbarry, aren't covered yet. There's no gauge for that water.
- The chart is read on an 11 m grid, so narrow cuts and bridge arches are approximate. Check them by eye.
- A green square on a pole is either a channel mark or a bridge height gauge, and the two look the same on the chart. If the route can't keep one to starboard, it lists that one for you to check.
